02 — THE INSTRUMENT

A mirror the size
of a small sea

Eleven hundred cold hexagons, grown in orbit and folded open like a night flower. Vesper does not take pictures. It holds its breath for years at a time, and lets the ancient light walk in.

03 — THE DEEP FIELD

Stare at nothing.
It becomes everything.

The patch of sky we watch is smaller than a grain of sand held at arm's length — and inside it, eleven thousand galaxies are burning quietly. Emptiness, it turns out, was never the right word.

05 — THE SIGNALS

The dark
is not silent

Pulsars keep time like lighthouses. Hydrogen hums at twenty-one centimetres. And once a decade, something we cannot name repeats itself — twice — and goes quiet. We log everything. We promise nothing.
